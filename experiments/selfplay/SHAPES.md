### `opcode_swap` (1 variant(s))

### `two_qubit_opcode_swap` (1 variant(s))

### `literal_rewrite` (2 variant(s))
- r2 constant truncated 0.70710678118 -> 0.7071
- r2 constant perturbed in the 9th decimal

### `theta_rescale` (2 variant(s))
- rx branch passes 2*theta to turn (theta/2 halving defeated)
- crx branch passes 2*theta to turn

### `phase_sign_flip` (1 variant(s))
- phaseturn drops the minus inside sin(-theta/2) for the y-component

### `boundary_offbyone` (2 variant(s))
- shot-sampling loop drops the final shot (range(shots) -> range(shots-1))
- single-qubit pair loop iterates one extra low index

### `noise_mixing_swap` (1 variant(s))
- measurement-error mixing weights inverted on both branches

### `comparison_flip` (1 variant(s))
- sampling acceptance r<cumu -> r<=cumu (boundary double-count risk)

### `measurement_map_swap` (1 variant(s))
- output-bit assembly writes out_list[bit] instead of out_list[qc.num_clbits-1-bit] (bit-order flip)

### `gate_noop` (1 variant(s))
- x gate body replaced with no-op self-swap (gate silently dropped)

### `init_state_perturb` (1 variant(s))
- all-|0> initialization becomes k[0]=[0.0,1.0] (imaginary amplitude)

### `return_value_perturb` (1 variant(s))
- probs drops the imaginary-amplitude contribution (e[0]**2 only)
