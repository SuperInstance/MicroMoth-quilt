Backend

By default everything above runs on a real (simulated) quantum circuit — Hadamard gates plus measurement — computed locally in your browser via MicroMoth, no network call and no key required. Paste a Moth Quantum API key (platform.mothquantum.com/keys) to attempt real Born-rule measurements on IBM quantum hardware instead. If that call can't complete for any reason, this demo says so and falls back to the simulated result automatically — you always get an answer.
